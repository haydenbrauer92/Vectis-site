My North Pole Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

My North Pole is a family Christmas activity app operated by Vectis Innovation Labs. The current beta is designed for use by families on their own devices. Parents should supervise children's use and decide what information and photos to enter.

Information on your device

The app stores family and child profiles, names, letters, wish lists, elf diary entries, captions, selected photos, preferences and local Santa photo replies on the device. It also stores a protected parent PIN verifier in the device Keychain. Photos are resized and saved locally. The app does not send letters or photos to Santa or an AI service; an adult prepares replies in Parent Mode using local templates. The current beta has no app account, cloud sync service, third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Camera, photos and biometrics

If you choose to use the camera or photo picker, the system lets you capture or select photos for wishes, letters, the elf diary or the gallery. You can decline access. If you choose biometric unlock, Apple’s system performs authentication; the app receives the result and does not receive your face or fingerprint template.

Sharing and external services

A parent can choose to export a photo using the system share sheet. The destination you select receives the shared photo and applies its own privacy practices. A parent can choose a shopping search or saved product link; the entered search terms or link are sent to the chosen website or Maps, which may process them under its own policy. Do not enter a child’s personal information as a shopping search term. Apple operates the App Store and TestFlight and may process account, installation, diagnostic or feedback information according to Apple’s policies. If you email support, your email provider and Vectis receive the details and attachments you send.

Backups and retention

Information remains on the device until you delete it, reset the demo content or remove the app, subject to device and Keychain behaviour. Apple-managed backups or device transfers may include app data depending on your settings; those copies are controlled through Apple and device settings. Exported copies and backups are outside the app and must be managed separately. Existing legacy data from earlier versions is preserved separately and is not automatically migrated into this beta.

Your choices and children’s privacy

Parents can edit or remove supported entries and photos in the app. Parent Mode offers a confirmed reset of local demo content; PIN recovery has a separate confirmed reset. Removing the app removes its local app container, but system backups, exported files and Keychain items may remain and should be managed separately. Permission can be changed in iOS Settings. We do not operate an account or server containing these local records, so we cannot retrieve or remotely delete them. Contact us for questions about children’s privacy or information you have sent to support.

Contact

Email support@vectisinnovationlabs.com. Please avoid sending children’s photos, PINs or other sensitive information unless necessary to resolve your request.

This policy describes the current local beta. If data practices change, the policy and relevant disclosures must be updated.
